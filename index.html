Standard LCD Display

An affordable replacement glass & backlit LCD assembly suitable for general usage.

  • Lowest Overall Cost: Keeps repair costs low while delivering full touch functionality.
  • Durable & Reliable: Good standard durability for standard daily tasks.
  • Tested Quality: Tested for touch accuracy, glass clarity, and backlight uniformity.
  • Slightly thicker borders and higher battery power draw than OLED displays.

Premium Soft OLED Display

Matches factory screen performance with vibrant true colors and slim original borders.

  • True OLED Contrast: Deep black levels, vivid colors, and high dynamic range.
